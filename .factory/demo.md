# Demo sandbox

- URL: `https://signal-school.sociobot.in/demo` (or local `/demo`).
- Sample: Tide Lines starts immediately with a Relay runner view, two named practice teammates, a populated four-node relay board, and three route decisions.
- Storage: the demo uses `demo:signal-school:run`. Real practice uses `signal-school:run`; settings use `signal-school:settings` and are not game progress.
- Reset: use **Reset demo** in the persistent `Demo — sample data, nothing is saved` banner. **Start for real** leaves the demo route and reads only the real-practice key.
- Network: the sample has no room controls, starts no room connection, and makes no cross-origin request. The online room service is contacted only from the real-practice page after a visitor creates or joins a room.
